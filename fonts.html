CrossPoint font browser

Every Latin, Cyrillic and Greek font CrossPoint 2.0 RC can use, the built-in Noto Sans plus 22 downloads, drawn on an Xteink X4 screen from the reader's own font files, kerning and line-breaking rules.

Fonts

    Zoom

    Screen to scale: 480 × 800 pixels, one canvas pixel per device pixel. The case outline is approximate. Grey levels follow CrossPoint's 2-bit anti-aliasing; the panel's real tones vary with lighting.

    Reader settings

    Defaults match a fresh CrossPoint install. Hyphenation and Focus Reading aren't simulated.

    Sample text

    One paragraph per line. *italic*, **bold**, ***bold italic***; \* for a literal asterisk. Characters a font doesn't include show as a replacement box, as on the reader.
