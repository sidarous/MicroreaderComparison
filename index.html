Tap a row or chip to toggle; hover to highlight. Button counts are physical keys; a rocker counts as two. "+ touch Home" is a capacitive key. "Not listed" means the maker doesn't mention a magnet. LilyGo's magnet is the MagSafe charging alignment ring. PPI uses published figures where available. ESP32-C3 devices have ~400 KB internal RAM and no external PSRAM. EEGO battery is the rated figure. LilyGo dimensions come from the T5 S3 Pro spec. Screens are drawn centered, so bezel positions are approximate. The thickness view shows each device from the side, laid flat, at the same scale as the Center and Bottom views.
